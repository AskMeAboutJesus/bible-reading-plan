# Privacy Policy

**Daily Bible Reading Plan**
Effective 12 November 2026

Daily Bible Reading Plan is made by Tom Wilson. This page explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

The app does not collect any information about you. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the app stores, and where

The app stores the reading plans you create, the days you mark as studied, and your settings (font size and preferred Bible app).

- **On your device.** All of this is saved on the device you are using.
- **In your own iCloud account.** If you are signed in to iCloud, your plans and studied days are also saved to your private iCloud database so that they stay in step across your iPhone, iPad and Mac. This storage belongs to your Apple Account and is provided by Apple. The developer cannot see or access it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's own privacy policy.

Font size and preferred Bible app are kept on each device and are not synced.

## What the developer receives

Nothing. The app does not send your plans, your progress or any other information to the developer or to any other party.

## Opening readings in other apps

When you tap a reading, the app opens that passage in the Bible app you chose in Settings. If that app is not installed, the passage opens on the web instead. Those apps and websites are run by other organisations and have their own privacy policies.

## Removing your information

- Delete a plan in the app to remove it from your device and from your iCloud account.
- Delete the app to remove everything it stored on that device.
- You can also remove the app's iCloud data in your device's iCloud storage settings.

## Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
