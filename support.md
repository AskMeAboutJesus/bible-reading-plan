# Support

**Daily Bible Reading Plan**

For help, questions or suggestions, write to [twappsupport@icloud.com](mailto:twappsupport@icloud.com). Please say which device you are using and what you were doing when the problem appeared.

## Common questions

### How do I create a reading plan?

Tap **New Plan** in the toolbar (the plus button). Give the plan a name, choose a start date, set how many Old Testament and New Testament chapters to read each day, and tap to create it.

### What does each day include?

One reading from each of four parts of Scripture: the Old Testament, Psalms, Proverbs and the New Testament.

### How do I open a reading in my Bible app?

Tap the reading. It opens in the Bible app chosen under **Settings**, then **Bible App**. If that app is not installed, the passage opens on the web instead.

### I fell behind. How do I catch up?

Use **Update Dates** in the toolbar. Choose the day you want to restart from and a new date, and every later day moves with it.

### What happens when my plan ends?

Use **Extend Plan** in the toolbar to add another year. Each of the four readings carries on from where it stopped.

### My plans are not appearing on my other device

- Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, with iCloud turned on for this app.
- Open **Settings** in the app and look at **iCloud Sync**. It shows whether sync is up to date, waiting, or off.
- Open the app on both devices while they are connected to the internet. Changes normally arrive within a minute or two.

The app works fully without iCloud. In that case your plans stay on the one device.

### Does the app work on a Mac?

Yes, on Macs with Apple silicon. Plans sync with your iPhone and iPad through iCloud.

### Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon.

### Does the app collect my information?

No. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
